ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Bringing local development closer to people.

Village & Panchayat Innovation (VPI) is an independent project concept focused on making it easier for people to engage with local development priorities.

The idea is simple: help residents share concerns and ideas, make local information easier to access, and support better coordination between communities and public institutions.
